Project 1 · Thesis: accelerated biological aging in students
Question: which factors (sex, sleep quality, mental health, physical activity) are linked to biological age running ahead of chronological age?
Data: primary survey of 599 UIN Jakarta students from 5 faculties (73% response rate), collected with KoboToolBox and checked against original forms.
Method: descriptive statistics and multiple logistic regression in SPSS.

What the model says
  • Only sex is significant: women have 1.43× the odds (OR 1.429, 95% CI 1.005–2.032, p = 0.047).
  • Poor sleep, mental disorder and lighter activity all point in the expected direction, but are not statistically significant.
  • Conclusion: the pattern is consistent with theory; larger and more varied samples are needed to confirm it.
